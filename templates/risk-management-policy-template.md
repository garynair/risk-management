## 1. Purpose

State why this policy exists and what it governs — typically, establishing the authority, scope, and structure of the organization's risk-management program.

## 2. Scope

- Business units, systems, and processes covered.
- Risk categories covered (per your taxonomy — see Section 4).
- Explicitly note anything out of scope and where it is governed instead (e.g., a separate BCP/DR policy, a separate information-security policy).

## 3. Methodology

- **Adopted framework:** (e.g., ISO 31000, COSO ERM, or a stated hybrid)
- **Risk assessment process:** identify -> analyze -> evaluate -> treat -> monitor -> communicate (or your adapted cycle)
- **Scoring scales:** reference the risk-heatmap-scoring-guide template rather than restating the scales here, so the two documents cannot drift out of sync.
- **Qualitative vs. quantitative analysis triggers:** state which risk tiers require full FAIR/quantitative analysis versus qualitative scoring only.

## 4. Risk Taxonomy

List your top-level risk categories (typically 6-8): e.g., Strategic, Operational, Financial, Compliance/Legal, Cyber/Technology, Third-Party, Reputational.

## 5. Governance and Roles (Three Lines Model)

- **First Line (Business/Operational Management):** responsibilities.
- **Second Line (Risk Management and Compliance):** responsibilities.
- **Third Line (Internal Audit):** responsibilities, and explicit statement of independence from remediation work.
- **Risk Committee / Executive Sponsor:** composition, authority, meeting cadence.
- **Board Oversight:** what the board reviews and how often.

## 6. Risk Appetite and Tolerance

- Reference the current, separately-approved Risk Appetite Statement by name/version.
- State who has authority to approve risk acceptance at each severity band (Low/Medium/High/Critical).

## 7. Risk Register Requirements

- Mandate that all identified risks are logged in the central risk register (reference the risk-register-template).
- Minimum required fields (reference the Risk Register section of this policy's supporting documentation).
- Requirement that every open risk carries an owner and a next-review date.

## 8. Risk Treatment

- State the four treatment options (Treat, Transfer, Tolerate, Terminate) and the approval authority required for each, scaled by severity.
- Reference the compensating-control-worksheet requirement for any control gap not remediated through a primary control.

## 9. Continuous Monitoring

- Requirement that top-tier risks have at least one linked Key Risk Indicator (KRI) with a defined threshold.
- Escalation path when a KRI breaches its threshold.

## 10. Review Cadence

State the mandatory review frequency for each artifact (reference the periodic-review-checklist template):
- Risk register: quarterly minimum.
- Risk appetite statement: annually, or after material strategic change.
- This policy: annually.
- Independent program assurance (internal audit): annually.

## 11. Exceptions

- Process for requesting a temporary exception to any requirement in this policy, who approves it, and the maximum duration before mandatory re-justification.

## 12. Definitions

- Risk, Issue, Inherent Risk, Residual Risk, KRI, KPI, Compensating Control, Risk Appetite, Risk Tolerance — define each as used in this policy to avoid drift in interpretation across business units.

## 13. Related Documents

- Risk Register
- Risk Appetite Statement
- RACI Matrix
- Compensating Control Worksheets (as issued)
- Business Continuity / Disaster Recovery Policy
- Information Security Policy
