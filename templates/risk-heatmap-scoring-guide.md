# Risk Heat Map Scoring Guide

Adapt the scale anchors and colour-band cut points to your own risk appetite statement before using this in a live programme — the numbers below are a reasonable, common starting point, not a universal standard.

## Likelihood Scale

| Score | Label | Anchor Definition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rare | Expected less than once every 5 years. |
| 2 | Unlikely | Expected once every 2-5 years. |
| 3 | Possible | Expected roughly once per year. |
| 4 | Likely | Expected more than once per year. |
| 5 | Almost Certain | Expected monthly or more frequently. |

## Impact Scale

| Score | Label | Anchor Definition (adapt figures to your organisation's size) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Negligible | No material financial loss; no regulatory or reputational impact. |
| 2 | Minor | Financial loss under [$X]; contained, no external disclosure required. |
| 3 | Moderate | Financial loss of [$X-$Y]; limited regulatory inquiry or customer-facing impact. |
| 4 | Major | Financial loss of [$Y-$Z]; mandatory regulatory notification or significant reputational damage. |
| 5 | Severe | Financial loss exceeding [$Z]; existential regulatory action, major customer attrition, or safety impact. |

## 5x5 Risk Score Grid (Likelihood x Impact)

| Likelihood \ Impact | 1 - Negligible | 2 - Minor | 3 - Moderate | 4 - Major | 5 - Severe |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **5 - Almost Certain** | 5 | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
| **4 - Likely** | 4 | 8 | 12 | 16 | 20 |
| **3 - Possible** | 3 | 6 | 9 | 12 | 15 |
| **2 - Unlikely** | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
| **1 - Rare** | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |

## Suggested Colour Bands

Set these cut points to match your documented risk appetite thresholds, not an arbitrary even split:

- **1-4 — Low (Green):** Accept at business-unit-owner level; review annually.
- **5-9 — Medium (Yellow):** Accept at senior-management level with documented rationale; review semi-annually.
- **10-14 — High (Orange):** Requires an active treatment plan; review quarterly; risk-committee visibility.
- **15-25 — Critical (Red):** Requires board or risk-committee approval to accept; active treatment plan mandatory; review monthly until reduced.

## How to Use This

1. Score every risk's **inherent** likelihood and impact (before controls) and plot it.
2. Score the same risk's **residual** likelihood and impact (after existing controls, including compensating controls) and plot it separately.
3. Label each plotted point with its Risk ID from the risk register — an unlabelled heat map cannot be traced back to an owner or a treatment plan.
4. Regenerate the map every review cycle (see the periodic review checklist) so movement between bands is visible over time.
